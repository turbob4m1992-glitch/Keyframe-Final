Case Study / Shopify + 3D

KLEOS+KLEA, skincare you can almost touch.

A custom Shopify store and a full library of photoreal 3D product renders for a natural skincare brand. One studio for the storefront and the product imagery, so every page, card, and ad shows the same bottle in the same light.

ClientKLEOS+KLEA/United States/kleosandklea.com ↗

The brief

KLEOS+KLEA makes whole-plant skincare built on more than two decades of ingredient research. The products are minimal, sculptural, and honest about what is inside them. The old store did not carry that: stock layouts, mismatched product photos, and a wholesale side bolted on. The founder wanted a store that felt as considered as the formulas, and product imagery that matched across every page.

What we built

  • A custom Shopify theme, not a template. Designed and coded for the brand: the homepage story, the Four Essentials lineup, the ritual in motion, ingredient philosophy, real-customer content, and the founder's story.
  • Seven product pages on four templates. The four core formulas, THALLO, the Discovery Set, and the Essential Collection bundle, each laid out from the founder's own page notes.
  • Built to sell and to keep selling. Subscribe-and-save, product reviews, email capture, and the professional wholesale portal all wired into the new theme.
  • Day and night mode. The store shifts with the time of day, and visitors can switch it themselves.
  • Owned by the client's team. Every word and photo is editable in the Shopify theme editor, with a written handover so any developer can pick it up.

The 3D product renders

We modelled the product line in 3D and rendered it photoreal: soft studio light for the store, a darker hero look for campaigns, extra camera angles, and 360-degree turntables that let the homepage lineup turn each bottle on hover. The Back Bar professional range got its own approved studio set, so wholesale buyers see the same quality as retail customers.

STUDIO AND DARK HERO RENDERS. NO PHOTO SHOOT, ONE CONSISTENT LIGHT.

Why it works

Skincare sells on trust, and trust comes from consistency. When the bottle in the ad, the bottle on the product page, and the bottle in the bag all look identical, the brand reads as precise before anyone reads a word. Doing the store and the renders in one studio meant the imagery was made for the layouts, not squeezed into them.

Frequently asked

What did Keyframe deliver for KLEOS+KLEA?+
A custom Shopify theme built from scratch for kleosandklea.com, plus photoreal 3D renders of the product line: studio and dark hero renders, extra camera angles, and 360-degree turntables for the homepage product lineup.
Is the site built on a Shopify template?+
No. The theme was designed and coded for the brand, with its own sections, seven product pages on four templates, and a day and night mode. The client's team edits every word and photo in the Shopify theme editor without touching code.
Why use 3D renders instead of a photo shoot?+
Renders give every product the same light, angle, and background, and new angles cost a re-render rather than a reshoot. The same files power the store photos, the homepage 3D turn, the Back Bar professional range, and marketing.
