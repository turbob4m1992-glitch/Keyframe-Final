- **Includes:**
  - Custom design & development (React / Next.js)
  - Up to 5 custom pages
  - High-performance mobile optimization (100/100 Core Web Vitals target)
  - Custom typography & brand color implementation
  - Basic SEO & GEO structured data setup
  - Delivery in 2 to 3 weeks

### 2. Cinematic Brand Build (from $8,000 USD)
- **Best for:** Premium GCC brands, luxury real estate, hospitality, and growing regional enterprises wanting an Awwwards-tier digital impression.

  - Advanced GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) & JSON-LD schema
  - Delivery in 6 to 10 weeks

### 3. Enterprise & Flagship Platform (from $15,000 USD)
- **Best for:** Large GCC luxury groups, international brands, and custom web applications.
- **Includes:**
  - Full-scale custom platform & e-commerce architecture

  - Founder-dedicated senior craft & ongoing retainer support
